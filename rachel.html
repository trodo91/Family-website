PROFILE

Ms RACHEL MAKAFUI KOTOKU.

Born on 7th September 1999, Rachel Makafui Kotoku is the second-born of the Kotoku family and a passionate finance professional with a strong drive for academic and professional excellence. With multiple qualifications in the field of finance, including a Masters degree, Rachel has built herself into a knowledgeable and ambitious woman with a clear vision for her future. Her journey does not end with her current achievements, as she plans to further her education by pursuing a PhD in Finance. Driven by ambition, continuous learning, and a desire to excel, Rachel represents the family's spirit of perseverance and intellectual growth. In short: An ambitious finance professional, accomplished scholar, and determined second-born with her sights set on a PhD and an even greater future in finance
