PROFILE

MR KWAME JOHN KOTOKU.

Born on December 17, Mr. Kwame John Kotoku is a retired banker who dedicated his professional career to the Bank of Ghana. Known for his strong principles, discipline, and strict moral values, he has always believed in integrity, responsibility, and doing what is right. Beyond his career, Mr. Kotoku is a devoted family from Vapko in the Volta Region, man whose values and life lessons continue to influence those around him. In short: A disciplined and principled retired banker, respected for his integrity, strong morals, and dedication to his family.
