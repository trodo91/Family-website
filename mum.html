PROFILE

MRS AGNES ABRA KOTOKU.

Born on 25th July, Mrs. Agnes Abra Kotoku is a retired Head of Treasury at the Parliament of Ghana, where she built a career marked by dedication, responsibility, and professionalism. Calm, composed, and warm-hearted, Mrs. Kotoku is the kind of person whose quiet presence brings a sense of peace to the family. She is known for her caring nature and the special way she makes everyone feel at home. And if there is one thing the Kotoku family will always agree on, it is this: Mrs. Agnes makes the best jollof! Her cooking has become one of those little things that makes family gatherings even more special. In short: A calm and accomplished woman, devoted to her family, with a remarkable career and an undeniable talent for making unforgettable jollof.
