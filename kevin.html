PROFILE

MR KEVIN JOHN ELIKEM KOTOKU

Born on 9th May 2002, Kevin John Elikem Kotoku is a young man with a wide range of interests, ambitions, and dreams for the future. He has a deep love for music, sports, and fitness, with the gym being one of his favourite places to stay active and challenge himself. Faith also plays an important role in Kevin's life. He is deeply rooted in his religion and considers his faith an important part of who he is and how he approaches life. What makes Kevin unique is the breadth of his ambitions. Rather than limiting himself to one path, he dreams of exploring several fields and professions, particularly health, technology, and engineering. His curiosity and willingness to learn reflect someone who is constantly thinking about what he can become and the impact he can make in the future. In short: A faith-driven, energetic, and ambitious young man with a passion for music, sports, fitness, and a future that could take him anywhere from healthcare to technology and engineering.
