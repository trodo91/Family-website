PROFILE

Ms JULIET SEYRAM KOTOKU.

Born on 17th January 1996, Juliet Kotoku is a Doctor of Pharmacy and the eldest of three siblings. As the firstborn, she naturally carries a strong sense of responsibility and takes her role in the family seriously. Juliet is known for being strict, determined, and focused. She sets high standards for herself and those around her, and when she sets her mind to something, she is not easily distracted from achieving it. Behind her serious and disciplined nature is someone who deeply values her family and wants the best for those around her. As the eldest sibling, she is often one of the people others can look to for guidance and direction. In short: A determined and disciplined pharmacist, proud firstborn, and a strong presence in the Kotoku family.
